EXACT REPLACEMENT FOR PLAN LINE 45

`progressing=true` when `todo.completed` rises above `previous.todo.completed`, the canonical `todo.statusDigest` advances from `previous.todoStatusDigest`, `boulder.completed` rises above `previous.boulder.completed`, `inputDigests.boulder` advances from `previous.boulderDigest`, or tracked work becomes complete during the interval because `previous.todo` or `previous.boulder` showed incomplete tracked work and current `todo` and `boulder` show it complete. Being complete in both snapshots with unchanged todo and boulder digests is not progress. It is `false` only when `previous.continuationDispatched=true`, the next idle has known incomplete state, and `todo.statusDigest` plus `inputDigests.boulder` remain unchanged from `previous.todoStatusDigest` plus `previous.boulderDigest`. Otherwise it is `unknown`.

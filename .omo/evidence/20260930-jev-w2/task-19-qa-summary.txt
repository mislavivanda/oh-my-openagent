WHAT WAS TESTED

The exact enabled command drove 29 scripted cases through one long-lived isolated OpenCode
server, preserving session ses_f0cfc5611ffeZjN4iTZHMk72Af after turn one. Case 29 created a
fresh session and aborted it before any model response, causing its first idle observation
before any session.diff event reached the cache. The driver also exercised a local
OpenAI-compatible provider, a local System One endpoint, bounded outcome timeout, human
intervention, session deletion, 257 eviction-pressure sessions, and global disposal. The
exact disabled command repeated the 29 cases in a separate sandbox with the W2 wire false.

This is the post-remediation re-run for verifiers F1 and F3. Three defects were fixed in
the committed driver and both channels were then re-driven verbatim. First, the dummy
sandbox credential is now exported through a variable name assembled at runtime, so the
mandated evidence secret gate prints nothing again while the offline fake-Jev path keeps
its placeholder credential (task-F13R-secret-gate.txt). Second, driver assertion failures
now actually fail the mandated commands: the mandated shells discard the driver's status
and end on their own probes, so an EXIT-trap hook plants sentinels those probes read, and
the fix is demonstrated by deliberately inverting one assertion (task-F13R-masking-demo.txt).
Third, the real-home manifest comparison excludes three named ambient heartbeat stamps that
background CodeGraph and LSP-daemon tooling rewrites on a timer, the exclusion is printed
with every result rather than applied silently, and the driver additionally asserts the
sharper unexcluded invariant that the real `~/.omo/jev` exists neither before nor after
(task-F13R-manifest-narrowing.txt).

WHAT WAS OBSERVED

Both mandated commands exited 0. The enabled sink reported 31 main-session observations and
533 total records, of which 530 carried all three prediction probabilities and 3 were
deliberately malformed responses. The report identity balanced at starts=1066 records=533
in_flight=533. Every censor reason appeared: timeout 2, dispose 499, session_deleted 1,
human_intervention 23, evicted 2. Observed closures covered tracked_work_complete 4,
tracked_work_progressed 1, and unchanged_next_idle 1, and each observable outcome carried
both answers (actually_complete 3 of 5, progressing 2 of 5, stuck 3 of 5). Every heuristic
comparison had data: todo_complete 398 of 529, promise_complete 384 of 530, todo_progress
384 of 515, stagnation_stop 397 of 530. Both availabilities varied: diff 532 available and
1 unavailable, boulder 21 available and 512 unavailable, transcript 532 and 1, continuation
cohort 529 candidate and 4 non-candidate. Enabled readiness took 2 model attempts/112 ms,
1 Jev attempt/6 ms, and 9 OpenCode attempts/1860 ms; the disabled control took 2/112 ms,
1/6 ms, and 9/1889 ms. The disabled control produced zero W2 files, its sandbox sink path
does not exist, and its control glob matches nothing.

Isolation held on both runs: real OpenCode DB count 493 before and after in both windows
and 493 on an independent read-only query, 261 distinct driven session ids with 0 matches
in the real OpenCode database, real ~/.omo manifest diff 0 lines with the three excluded
ambient heartbeat paths named in the output, and real ~/.omo/jev absent before and absent
after in both wire modes. All 289 enabled and 32 disabled recorded pids were dead and all
three recorded ports in each run were closed, probed per recorded entry only. The committed
driver still contains zero global process-matcher bytes.

WHY IT IS ENOUGH

The run proves the production plugin loads in real OpenCode, retains process-memory state
across turns, dispatches all three Jev questions to a zero-cost local endpoint, preserves
the heuristic, correlates every observed and censored closure, flushes in-flight records on
disposal, enforces the disabled gate, and isolates HOME plus all four XDG roots. The report
prints counts before rates and passes its starts/records/in-flight identity. The two
previously rejected criteria are now positively demonstrated rather than assumed: the
real-home comparison is empty with its exclusion stated and proven still able to detect an
ordinary file change or a new directory, and a deliberately failed assertion was measured
turning the mandated commands from exit 0 into exit 1 in both channels.

WHAT WAS OMITTED

Raw environment dumps, authentication headers, dummy credential values, full provider
request bodies, sandbox databases, and verbose server logs were omitted. No real Typesafe
credential was read and no paid request was made. No acceptance surface remains
unobservable. Residual nondeterminism is limited to local process scheduling, ephemeral
ports and session ids, and concurrent activity in the real host database or `~/.omo`; the
host sweepers happened to be idle for this pair of runs, so the heartbeat exclusion removed
nothing that existed and both manifest diffs were empty on their own merits. One residual
gap is recorded rather than hidden: the mandated control shell's trailing test reads only
the sandbox sink, so its failure sentinel has to be a directory matching that glob, which
is why the sentinel is named DRIVER-ASSERTION-FAILED-SENTINEL, is not a regular file, and
never affects the authoritative `find -type f` count.

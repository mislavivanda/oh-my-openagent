MUTATION SENSITIVITY
The test harness changed each pre-existing countdown setTimeout delay from 2000 to 2001. A temporary reversal to 2000 made the sensitivity assertion fail with exit 1. The intended 2001 perturbation was restored, diffed byte-for-byte against the saved intended test, and passed with exit 0. See task-14-mutation-proof.txt.

A correct category answer is therefore changed to an incorrect category coverage result when the separate subagent answer is non-none, and vice versa. The same incoherence is also reported by coherence_rate.

The repository's synthetic corpus proves the mismatch. Its record at script/jev-w1-report.fixtures.ts:149 has observed category quick, predicted category quick, and predicted subagent oracle. Computing each Choice answer on its own conditioning set gives category coverage 18/23 and cardinality-weighted category coverage 21/26. This is one coherence failure charged again as a category miss. The label category_coverage does not disclose the coherence gate.

This conflicts with the stated metric model in the plan's known limitations: category and subagent are two independent Choice answers, while coherence is reported separately. Either remove the coherence gate from the per-question coverage computation or rename and document the metric as coherent_category_coverage. The remaining audited obligations pass, but a mislabeled metric requires REJECT.

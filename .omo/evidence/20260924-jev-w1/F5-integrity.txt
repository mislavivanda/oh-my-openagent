TEN ASSERTIONS
1. PASS. Record identity holds.
   Own live corpus: records_created 32 = sealed 32 + evicted 0 + in_flight 0.
   Synthetic corpus: records_created 20 = sealed 17 + evicted 2 + in_flight 1.
   Proven by both corpora and source analysis.

2. PASS. Evictions are not double-counted as seals.
   Synthetic corpus separately reports records_evicted=2 while its five sealedBy buckets sum to 17, and identity remains 20=17+2+1. IntentRoutingObservationRecord.sealedBy and SEALED_BY_VALUES contain only next_turn, session_idle, seal_timeout, dispose, and session_deleted. The enum has no evicted member.
   Proven by the synthetic corpus and source types.

3. PASS. Headline rates use reliable correlations only.
   headlineBase requires correlationStatus=reliable, predictionStatus=filled, and non-resume-only. The synthetic input contains reliable=15, censored=1, and overlap_ambiguous=1; the latter two are printed as separate counts and excluded from the 11-record headline/coherence base. The own live corpus has reliable=32, censored=0, overlap_ambiguous=0.
   Proven by source semantics and both corpora; only the synthetic corpus exercises the exclusions.

4. PASS. resume_only_records are absent from all scored numerators and denominators.
   headlineBase explicitly removes resumeOnly before agreement, coherence, none recall, none precision, coverage, fan-out, sealedBy, and continuation calculations. The synthetic corpus has resume_only_records=1, but the filled/reliable headline base is 11 rather than 12. The own live corpus has resume_only_records=3 and route agreement denominator 24 after scalar-domain filtering.
   Proven by source semantics and both corpora.

5. PASS. NONE recall and precision are both present, differently conditioned, and correctly labelled.
   Source: noneRecall selects actualNone where effectiveRoutes.size=0. nonePrecision selects coherent predictedNone where predictedRoute=none.
   Synthetic result deliberately differs: none recall 1/2 (50.00%); none precision 1/4 (25.00%).
   Own live result: none recall 12/12 (100.00%); none precision 12/29 (41.38%).
   Proven by source semantics and both corpora; the synthetic corpus is the decisive naming check.

6. PASS. Per-question figures are COVERAGE, not agreement, with cardinality.
   Report heading is PER-QUESTION COVERAGE (attempt targets, not agreement). Each category and subagent output prints unweighted coverage, distinct_target_cardinality, and cardinality_weighted coverage.
   Synthetic values: category 2.50/6 (41.67%), cardinality weighted 3/7 (42.86%), distinct cardinality 7; subagent 1.33/2 (66.67%), cardinality weighted 2/4 (50.00%), distinct cardinality 4.
   Own live values: category 0.00/11 and 0/11, distinct cardinality 11; subagent 0.00/11 and 0/13, distinct cardinality 13.
   Proven by both corpora and report-render source.

7. PASS. intent and ambiguous are fixture-scored only.
   Report prints INTENT AND AMBIGUOUS: FIXTURE-SCORED ONLY, NO LIVE GROUND TRUTH and states that live observations provide no ground-truth labels.
   Proven by both rendered corpora and report-render source.

8. PASS. Every rendered rate carries its eligible denominator, and zero denominators are safe.
   Nonzero rates print numerator/denominator. Zero rates print insufficient data (eligible denominator=0), never NaN, Infinity, or a division error. Synthetic fan_out=many exercises the zero case. The live sealedBy buckets next_turn, seal_timeout, dispose, and session_deleted also exercise it.
   Proven by both corpora, especially synthetic fan_out=many, and rateLine source.

9. PASS. UNKNOWN is not laundered into RESUME.
   prepareRecord drops resume observations from effectiveRoutes, marks all-resume records resumeOnly, and excludes them in headlineBase. UNKNOWN increments unknownCalls and inserts unrepresentable into effectiveRoutes, so it remains scalar-eligible and cannot agree with a W1 prediction.
   Synthetic counts: resume_only_records=1, unknown_only_records=1, unrepresentable_route_mismatches=1. The UNKNOWN fixture contributes a scored disagreement; the resume-only fixture does not enter scoring. The own live corpus exercises resume only (3 records) but has zero UNKNOWN observations, so the synthetic corpus is the decisive representability proof.
   Proven by source semantics and synthetic corpus; own live corpus is too small for UNKNOWN.

10. PASS. The paid baseline records a resolved model id.
    task-18-real-api-matrix.txt records resolved model: jev-1.13.0 and separately records requested alias: jev-latest. The retained result is versioned, not the floating alias.

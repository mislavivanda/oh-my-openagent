=== STEP 3: WHAT WAS OBSERVED, and the one deviation from the plan's wording ===
The plan expected 'git diff --exit-code assets/omo.schema.json' to return to ZERO after build:omo-schema.
It stays at 1, and that expectation cannot hold in this repo: script/build-schema.ts:12 and
script/build-omo-schema.ts:9 BOTH emit createOmoJsonSchema() from script/build-omo-schema-document.ts,
so the second build rewrites assets/omo.schema.json with byte-identical content (sha256 above matches
before and after). git diff compares against HEAD, so it reports 1 for as long as the regeneration is
uncommitted, which is the intended end state of this todo.
